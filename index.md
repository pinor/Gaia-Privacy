# Privacy Policy — Gaia Investing

Last updated: July 24, 2026

## Summary

Gaia is a personal investing journal. All your data stays in your own iCloud account. The developer has no access to it and no servers of their own.

## Data the developer collects

None. Gaia does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server operated by the developer. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

## Data you create in the app

When you use Gaia, the app creates records such as investment theses, catalysts, risks, plays, events, sector notes, and earnings analyses. These records are stored in two places on your device:

1. Locally, in the app's on-device storage.
2. In your private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit service, so your data syncs between your own Apple devices.

The developer cannot read, query, or export the contents of your private iCloud database. iCloud syncing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

## Third parties

Gaia does not send data to any third party. The only network service the app uses is Apple's CloudKit, which is required for iCloud sync and is subject to Apple's own privacy terms.

## Children's privacy

Gaia is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
